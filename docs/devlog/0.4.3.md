# Sanctum 0.4.3 — rests that actually rest

Small update, but it fixes something that had been quietly annoying me for weeks.

**Long rests work now.** The hit dice tracker was keeping its own private copy of your HP, hit dice and exhaustion. So you'd hit "Long Rest", the tracker would update, and the HP bar right above it would just... sit there on the old number until you reloaded the page. Two parts of the same screen disagreeing about how much health you had. Everything now reads from one source and refreshes together, and the rest buttons stay disabled while it saves so you can't double-tap your way into a mess.

While I was in there: a long rest gives back **all** your hit dice, not half. I know that's not RAW. But at level 3 "half your hit dice" means one die, and pressing a button to get one die back doesn't feel like a night's sleep. If people hate it I'll make it an option.

**Darkvision is a real field.** It used to be something you typed into your features box and hoped you'd remember. Now it's a checkbox in the combat section (defaults to 60 ft), shows up as a stat tile on the character overview, gets its own Senses panel in the exported PDF, and survives a round-trip through export/import.

**Background got its own home.** "Personality & Background" was one section doing two unrelated jobs — the four personality characteristics on one hand, your actual background on the other. Split them up: Characteristics keeps traits/ideals/bonds/flaws, Background holds the background picker (moved out of Identity) and a proper **backstory box**. The exported sheet had been dumping your notes into the standard Backstory field because there was nowhere else to put them; now backstory goes where backstory goes and notes get their own field.

That's it. Grab the portable exe or the installer below.
